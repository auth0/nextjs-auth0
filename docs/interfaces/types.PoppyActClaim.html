    Interface PoppyActClaim

    The act claim of a Personal Agent's ID token, as Auth0 sent it. The SDK requires sub, poppy_sid and poppy_user_id; Auth0 may send more claims than are listed here, and they are kept too.

    Beta and subject to change.
